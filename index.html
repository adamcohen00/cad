How to use

  • Distance: click two points.
  • Path: click points along a run (a fence, a wall); double-click or Enter to finish.
  • Area: click the corners; click the first point, double-click or press Enter to close.
  • Hold Shift to snap the angle to the sheet axes or the building/pool grid (≈11.3°).
  • Points snap to existing measurement points. Drag any point to adjust it.
  • Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Esc cancels, ⌫ removes the last point.
